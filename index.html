How I work

Less ceremony. More clarity.

The methods change with the problem. These principles generally don't.

Start with the problem, not the requested solution.

Understand why something is needed before deciding what to build.

Learn from the people closest to the work.

Metrics tell you where to look. Observation and conversation often tell you why.

Build the smallest useful system.

Solve today's problem while understanding where the capability needs to go next.

Make complexity visible, not bureaucratic.

Good program management should reduce coordination overhead, not create more of it.

Measure what happens after launch.

Adoption, behavior, performance, and feedback determine whether something actually worked.
